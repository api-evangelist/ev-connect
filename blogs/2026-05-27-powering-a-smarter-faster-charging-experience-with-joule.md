EV Connect has launched Joule, an AI-assisted chatbot integrated into its mobile app that delivers real-time support to EV drivers without requiring phone calls. The tool handles approximately 43% of daily support conversations by providing actionable assistance, transaction visibility, and network-specific guidance to improve charging session completion rates.
