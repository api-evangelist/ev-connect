EV owners save approximately $1,400 annually on fuel costs compared to gas drivers, with home charging costing roughly $694 per year versus $2,122 for gasoline. The federal EV tax credit expired in September 2025, but state incentive programs and manufacturer deals now offer significant savings, while the expanded public charging network of 250,000+ ports makes road trips increasingly practical.
