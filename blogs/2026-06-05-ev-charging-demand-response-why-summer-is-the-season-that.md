This article examines how EV charging demand response, particularly during summer peak periods, helps stabilize an increasingly strained electrical grid. Automated demand response (ADR) technology using OpenADR standards allows utilities to automatically reduce or shift EV charging loads during grid stress events, transforming EV charging networks into a distributed demand-management resource that improves grid resilience.
